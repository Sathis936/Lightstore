Plan by Space

Room-by-room lighting notes

Start with how the room is used, then choose a focal fixture that supports the layers of light around it.

Entry & Foyer

Make the arrival feel balanced

Use the room-sizing calculator as a starting point,from both the entrance and upper landings. In a double-height foyer, account for the full drop and keep circulation clear beneath.

Dining Room

Light the table, not the sightline

Center a chandelier over the table rather than the whole room. Choose a dimmable warm-white source and check seated eye-level sightlines so guests can see one another comfortably.

Living & Bedroom

Layer for comfort

Pair a decorative centrepiece with reading or task lights and softer indirect sources. Use separate dimming zones to shift from practical daytime light to a relaxed evening atmosphere.

Before you choose a fixture

  • Measure the room, table, ceiling height, and intended hanging point.
  • Confirm ceiling support and electrical capacity for the fixture weight and controls.
  • Match colour temperature and dimming to the room’s materials and everyday use.
  • Check the manufacturer’s installation instructions and use a qualified electrician.
